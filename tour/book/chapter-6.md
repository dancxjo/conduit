# Keep one Body through change

The machinery that realizes an intended computer does not stay still. A laptop reboots, a browser disappears, a Pico disconnects, or one part is replaced. If the computer's identity is merely the identity of today's processes and devices, continuity disappears with them.

## Conduit idea

The Body born on page zero is the durable computer Conduit is maintaining, distinct from its current Hosts, Boots, Plans, and Plays. Returning to earlier pages changes only what the Crèche presents; it does not perform another BIRTH.

## What the run proves

Use Previous, Next, or revisit page zero. The same Body and BIRTH Sign remain because those controls change presentation state, not Body lifecycle truth. This bounded lesson proves that separation; it does not manufacture evidence of a browser restart or absent physical Host returning.

## Payoff

Body identity is the continuity to maintain while machinery comes, goes, and is replanned. The intended computer can remain the same computer even when the current mechanism realizing it changes.

The Crèche began by birthing one Body. Its meaning does not have to be rewritten for each machine that helps make it real.
