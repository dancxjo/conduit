# Keep one Body through change

The machinery that realizes an intended computer does not stay still. A laptop reboots, a browser disappears, a Pico disconnects, or one part is replaced. If the computer's identity is merely the identity of today's processes and devices, continuity disappears with them.

## Conduit idea

A Body born in the Crèche is the durable computer Conduit maintains, distinct from its current Hosts, Boots, Plans, and Plays. Opening or navigating the Book performs no BIRTH and owns no lifecycle state.

## What the run proves

Use Previous and Next or reopen the Book. Those controls only navigate documentation; they neither inspect nor mutate Body lifecycle truth. The Crèche and compatible readers are responsible for projecting actual Body evidence.

## Payoff

Body identity is the continuity to maintain while machinery comes, goes, and is replanned. The intended computer can remain the same computer even when the current mechanism realizing it changes.

The Crèche can birth one Body without turning the Book into that Body's stateful shell. Its meaning does not have to be rewritten for each machine that helps make it real.
