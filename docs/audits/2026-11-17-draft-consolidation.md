response and dispatch additions are additionally test-only. Unsupported full

protocol tests, compiler resource limits, runtime grants, physical deployment and

coordination handoffs. Combined-source results belong to the consolidation PR;
prior counts are not relabeled as proof of this new tree. Raw logs and manifests
remain outside tracked source under ignored target output or the coordination
