# History maintenance with machine-writer policy

A source-history migration is a separate operator maintenance operation. The
normal train lander deliberately uses non-forced updates and refuses a policy
that permits force pushes or deletion. Its App has administration read access;
the independent policy observer is read-only. Neither credential nor the update
ruleset bypass authorizes a rewrite through the ordinary controller.

This procedure describes preparation and acceptance requirements. It registers
no App, changes no policy or ref, and does not replace explicit maintenance
authorization or the final source-preservation review. Keep ordinary development
and automated main publication under their existing contracts.

## Inactive bootstrap path

A separately authorized operator migration can precede App registration while
machine-writer policy and cheap admission remain inactive. First complete the
ordinary bootstrap Candidate, landing, exact dev-push Integration and publication.
Then settle writers and satisfy the same final backup, map, lease, event and
new-source acceptance requirements below. Do not activate the App merely to
authorize maintenance.

Save and restore the actual existing server gates and admission state. An
inactive installation must not silently acquire a future machine-only policy
as part of restoration. Dedicated App registration, independent actor-gate
proof, trusted mapped-source controller review and a real positive train remain
required before later machine activation. Historical mapping does not transfer
old live checks or products to a new source identity.

## Freeze and preserve the final source

With an active machine writer, finish the accepted train, actual dev-push
adoption and publication before freezing history. Drain controller sessions,
queued landers, releases and
synchronization work. Stop new admission, token issuance and dispatch; settle
ambiguous operations before revoking outstanding scoped tokens. Record the
admission variable's exact absent/present state before temporarily disabling
cheap input admission.

Coordinate every active source owner, including hardware work. Freeze branch,
release and tag writers, not only dev. Never reset an active owner's checkout or
reinterpret a running physical proof as execution of rewritten source. Retain
its original source, Body/Boot identity and evidence.

Before temporary policy changes, write an external maintenance intent containing:

- Actual repository/operator identities and complete current protection,
  ruleset, activation and controller observations, with exact restore payloads.
- The final remote/local/worktree union, including index and untracked source,
  immutable backup/map asset identities and independent restore verification.
- An explicit allowlist of affected refs, expected old OIDs, pure mapped targets,
  actual cutover targets, reverse leases and partial-failure handling.
- The writer-exclusive maintenance boundary, normal-policy readbacks, event
  handling and publication hold. Keep credentials out of this record.

Historical backups and earlier clean-worktree inventories are insufficient.
Refresh all refs and drift checks inside the freeze. Preserve all unselected
source/data and original objects before retiring advertised ancestry. Private
conversations, credentials and unrelated personal data are not public shipment
inputs.

Re-audit every affected tag's type, signature and associated release at this
final snapshot. An earlier observation that tags were lightweight or releases
mutable does not cover newly published releases. An affected immutable tag or
release blocks its proposed retarget. Do not delete/recreate releases or invent
an immutability bypass; review the scope or a separately authorized successor.

## Keep pure mapping separate from policy activation

First verify the pure rewritten graph, complete commit/ref/tree maps and strict
preservation of every unselected blob. Publish the immutable final map and
original backup. Then use the supported
[policy constructor](history-migration.md) to generate and review the final
capture policy against those exact assets and actual original/mapped objects.

Runtime historical validation reads only
`docs/audits/history-capture-lineage.json` committed at publication HEAD. An
external constructor output is not installed policy. Add that file as a
separately authored child of pure mapped dev. Check its single parent, exact
single-file delta, unchanged other source and all required capture relations.
The map cannot contain its own future policy or map-asset hashes.

Record both `pureMapTarget` and `actualCutoverTarget` in the final ref manifest.
Dev's cutover target includes the reviewed activation child; do not relabel it as
the original dev commit's mapped entry. Record its added policy blob, trees and
commit separately from the removal-only preservation proof. Main and historical
refs retain their reviewed pure mapping unless a separate change was reviewed.
Fresh automated publication can subsequently carry the policy into main.

Original capture IDs remain original. Historical mapping authorizes neither
live train ancestry nor reuse of a pre-rewrite check, manifest, run/attempt or
product as acceptance for a different source identity.

## Update exact refs and restore normal gates

Use separately reviewed operator maintenance authority; do not increase the
production App's permissions or add a human fallback inside the lander. The
independent classic proof/force gate, machine update ruleset and main policy
need intentional handling. A bypass of one is not authority over another.
Apply only the reviewed temporary allowances and verify their effective
readbacks before updating refs. Unknown actors, unrelated permission increases
or unexplained movement stop the operation.

Prefer one supported atomic push with explicit leases for the reviewed refs.
Never mirror-push blindly or update GitHub-owned pull refs. If atomic update is
unavailable, use the reviewed partial-failure journal. A transport error is not
a retry grant: inspect actual refs, retain the freeze and resolve ambiguity.
Compare every resulting ref with its intended actual cutover target.

Restore complete normal writer/proof/main gates and verify effective readbacks
before normal mutation resumes. If machine policy was active, the update bypass
must again name only the reviewed dedicated App; classic proof remains independent
and admin-enforced. The inactive path restores its saved ordinary gates.
Restore admission activation only to its recorded state when its live gates
remain valid. Migrate clean local refs/checkouts with exact old-OID checks and
owner handoff; do not merge original ancestry back or discard index evidence.

## Preserve events and hold publication writes

Drain old concurrency queues. Keep both the Integration push handler and the
Publish workflow-run handler enabled across the cutover. Use the reviewed
operator/App push channel that actually emits downstream events; an ordinary
workflow GITHUB_TOKEN push suppresses them. Capture the new actual dev-push run
ID, source and attempt. A missing run is a stop condition, not permission to
substitute manual dispatch or claim adoption.

The server maintenance boundary must block normal main, release-branch and tag
writes until normal policy restoration is verified, while allowing handler
execution and artifact reads. Do not rely on slow builds to enforce ordering.
The first mapped source cannot adopt an old source's train proof; it executes
fresh exhaustive Integration.

Publish currently has only the Integration-completed workflow-run trigger.
Disabling it through completion can lose that event. If its captured job refuses
because the maintenance hold is still active, inspect the concrete failure and
any retained PR/ref/draft assets. After restoring policy, resume only that
existing publication job against the same successful actual push run and sealed
products, within artifact retention. The publisher reauthenticates source and
products and verifies resumable state; mismatches refuse. Do not rerun exhaustive
Integration merely to manufacture a publication event or reset mismatched state.

## Prove the result or recover explicitly

Require exact new-source preflight, retained-evidence/site validation, actual
dev-push Integration and publication under restored policy. Renew the trusted
controller/configuration review at its new identity. Later train inputs need
fresh Candidate, ready-selection, exhaustive proof, landing and adoption custody.
Compiler data remains separate from behavioral/product authority.

Perform a real ordinary unfiltered network clone. Verify intended heads/tags,
complete maps, all unselected source bytes/modes, absence of selected generated
blobs, no alternate/promisor shortcuts and full fsck. Measure actual downloaded
and allocated bytes and clone time against the retained baseline. Synthetic
pack savings and server-owned pull-ref storage are separate observations.

A failure restores nothing automatically. Keep originals, journals and maintenance
ownership; record exactly which refs/policies changed and prepare a concrete
recovery intent. Reverse updates use original objects and leases against actual
post-cutover OIDs. Never force rollback over drift. Restore and read back normal
policy even when source rollback is needed, then verify the resulting exact
source. Preserve original backups, maps, release assets and physical evidence.
