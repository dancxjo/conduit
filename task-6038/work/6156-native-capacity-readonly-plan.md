## Why old86a is not a complete answer

It increases Mask/snapshot to256KiB, separates48KiB present results, enlarges the interaction-input queue ceiling and switches snapshot storage to preparation-preallocated HostedValueStore (avoiding a much larger inline FixedValueStore). Those are useful ideas, not reviewed current application. It does not establish complete96KiB observer handling, remote60/64KiB logical responses,16chunk reception, actual Source/Show/observer envelopes or a selected explicit current profile. Do not cherry-pick it wholesale.
