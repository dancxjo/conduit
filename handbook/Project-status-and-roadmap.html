Project status and roadmap

Conduit is experimental. The development tree includes checked Conduitese, immutable planning, one bounded kernel, body lifecycle, hosted/browser/ConduitOS execution, and environment-specific evidence. Those are different proof claims, not a promise of identical support on every host.
