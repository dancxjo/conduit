The idea

Meaning first. Placement later. Evidence always.

FormPortable semantic intent with typed ports and finite configuration.
OffersHosts truthfully advertise capabilities, resources, limits, and implementations.
PlanA planner seals one exact realization, including placement and link facts.
PlayBounded kernels execute the admitted fragments without inventing new semantics.
EvidencePresentation and lifecycle receipts prove what actually happened.
One model, many machines

A browser, a workstation, and a Pico can all be peers.

01 / std

Native hosts

Plan and execute ordinary work with hosted resources while retaining exact Conduit identities and bounds.

02 / wasm

Browser hosts

Rust/WASM kernels receive planned values over bounded WebSocket sessions and complete real DOM presentations.

03 / rp2040

Embedded hosts

A physical Pico W runs generated bounded fragments, presents through GPIO, and speaks the same session semantics over USB CDC.
