There is no process-global cache or growth beyond four.

Hits return a borrowed proof and still execute all subsequent original context/Source/Native admissions. Misses execute unchanged full ancestry/root/StateProof admission. No child law or disagreement is dropped.

Every proof has fixed ancestry, roots, and RawState arrays. Heap ownership is precisely the actual capacity of basis.analysis_revision/source_revision/text and each of eight relation subtype Strings. Ordinary Native input encoding likewise remains outside it; this is not a whole-parser finite storage claim.
