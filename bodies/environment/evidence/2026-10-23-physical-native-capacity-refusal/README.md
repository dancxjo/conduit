bytes, with a 93,724-byte peak. The old additional guard required 27,660 bytes;
only 24,852 remained. Root reported retained native possession separately from
its unprepared kernel. The Body stayed unstarted and no climate dispatch occurred.

capture. This is negative physical evidence, not a successful installation or
