# Native sampler after compact preparation and linked schemas

A clean ESP32 image completed three physical one-minute DHT observations with
exact Source Face replay, authenticated lifecycle acknowledgements, release,
quiescence, retirement and Body Lull. Root retained and peaked at 80,572 bytes
at the recorded sampling checkpoints, returning to 9,756 after retirement.
The earlier capture retained and peaked at 90,760 bytes: the 10,188-byte
reduction includes accumulated input-location compilation, narrowed payload
slots, composer schema sharing and linked schema borrowing. It does not isolate
one transformation. SDK remained at 33,408 bytes at those checkpoints, with
34,024 captured peak. These sampled counters are not signed heap attestation,
continuous monitoring, stack high water or worst-case wireless admission.

The linked stack reserves 36,908 bytes; stack usage was not measured. Source
identities, image receipt, telemetry checkpoints and capture hashes are retained
in sampling.json. Full image, map and captures remain outside Cargo targets in
`outputs/environment-native-face-probe/1792854764`.

The protected working image was restored and verified with twelve acknowledged
display updates across fresh sequences 1–3. This is a finite native sampler/Face
proof. Combined Mask/output execution, concurrent AP/client/fallback transitions,
durable recorder storage and physical overnight reliability remain unproven.
