# Committed environmental publication

`cargo xtask check device-protocols` includes the publication and prepared
canonical-input controls. Publication is a local runtime Back over already
produced values. It performs no sensor operation, network delivery or durable
storage effect, and does not establish Host readiness or a Body Play.

The four contracts keep readings, partial timeout captures, provider failure and
geometry refusal distinct. Readings retain the observation's first-class
Temperature and relative-humidity quantities. Publication copies canonical bytes;
unit conversion, rounding, fitting and presentation belong to a downstream Mask.

Preparation admits finite event capacity and backing derived from the complete
checked Type, capped by the channel envelope. Input validation checks the complete
canonical envelope and exact Type; leaf semantics remain with their owning
codecs. A staged value is invisible until the kernel commits its consumption.
Failed, cancelled or abandoned staging preserves the previous committed snapshot
and releases any shared sequence reservation. Committed outcomes share one local
order; that sequence is neither a clock nor a cross-Boot identity.

Execution, commit and cancellation allocate and reallocate nothing after
preparation. Controls exercise all four contracts, malformed and wrong-typed
input, capacity/fuel refusal, cancellation/drop, zero-event admission, commit
ordering, and the full quantity coordinate width. They are CPU conformance,
not physical sensor, browser delivery, recording persistence or overnight proof.
