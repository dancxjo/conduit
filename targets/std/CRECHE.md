# Scoped Crèche execution

The installed std profile contains the ordinary `creche/prepare` Back. The default
Host does not advertise it. `StdHost::new_with_creche_encounter` installs its
offer with one explicit encounter resource, without granting build, source,
toolchain, network or device authority.

`run_body_plan_with_creche_to_with_start` attaches a `HostedCrecheAdapter` to one
exact sealed Body Plan. It prepares the adapter with actual Host/Boot/generation,
producing Plot/Plan, prospective Body Play, resource and finite limits. The
request digest binds this context to the retained encounter admission envelope.
Interactive edits still need canonical review and admission before make effects;
the envelope does not authorize an arbitrary future selection.

The entry supplies the exact typed request to the planned external Fore. The
adapter cannot begin until lifecycle start and the start callback have succeeded
and the matching issued Play has been confirmed. An ordinary Body entry without
the selected owner refuses before Play. The kernel retains one exact pending
Host Call; it does not add an encounter scheduler or repeat effects on pressure.

Adapters prepare finite storage and work before Play. Their begin/poll operations
are nonblocking. Poll count, minimum poll interval and retained evidence byte
bound are explicit and committed into the request. The Body runner waits at most
10 ms at a time while a Crèche operation is pending, so cancellation and other
input can progress. The owner hashes retained evidence and checks its request
identity and byte bound before completing the typed outcome. Evidence bytes
remain in adapter custody after retirement. A digest proves byte custody, while
the concrete trusted adapter must establish canonical receipt validity and the
truth of its disposition.

Cancellation drains adapter-owned platform work before requesting hard kernel
cancellation. Preparation/start refusals also drain. A failed drain remains a
cleanup failure and prevents a successful/cancelled report; it is not evidence
that workers or external effects have stopped. The caller retains the adapter
and must handle that failure. Explicit retirement is required; Drop only makes
a final drain attempt and cannot report its result.

The focused proof parses the shipped `plots/creche/main.conduit`, plans with the
explicit offer, seals an ordinary Body Plan, starts its lifecycle, dispatches the
installed Back through the generic kernel Host Call, and acknowledges its typed
Fore output. Fixture adapters cover refusal, custody corruption, missing owner,
start refusal, operator stop and failed drain. These tests do not establish a
native interactive product, actual image manufacture, deployment, boot or join.
The concrete product adapter and supported product entrance remain to be wired.
