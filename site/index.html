The Body is the computer.

One logical computer.
One machine or many.

Conduit lets you make a Body, one logical computer, from the physical and virtual computers you actually have. A Body can be one multicore machine or a changing collection of browsers, laptops, VMs, Raspberry Pis, microcontrollers, and other Hosts.

Its programs are Forms: semantic graphs that describe what should happen without welding that meaning to the machines that happen to realize it now.

A small mental model

Programs are Forms. The Body carries the work.

Form
A program: connected semantic work.
Body
The logical computer, made from one Host or many.
Host
One current running environment offering finite machinery.
Plan
One exact admitted realization of the Body's current work.

A Body may run many Forms. The architectural direction is one logical scheduler per Body, planning all current work together rather than creating a separate scheduling universe for every program.
